Heading Level 1 (Main Title)

This is the most important heading on a page. Use only one per page.

Heading Level 2 (Section Title)

This is used for major sections of your content.

Heading Level 3 (Sub-section)

This breaks down information under an H2 heading.

Heading Level 4 (Sub-sub-section)

Used for deeper sub-sections or minor topics.

Heading Level 5 (Minor Heading)

Rarely used, but helpful for deep structural nesting.

Heading Level 6 (Smallest Heading)

This is the lowest level heading available in HTML.
